MOTION (in the app)
  Split-flap: top half falls, bottom half drops in. 300 ms per flip.
  Logo flips in on open and again after the page has been idle; SDX digits flip only when the score changes.
  Motion is switched off for people who turn on "reduce motion" on their phone.

Use on light backgrounds as-is (the tiles carry their own dark background). Don't recolour individual tiles or stretch the logo.
